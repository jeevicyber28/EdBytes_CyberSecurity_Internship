# Milestone 1: Announce Your EdBytes Internship on LinkedIn

## Ready-to-Post LinkedIn Draft (Copy & Paste)

```text
I am starting a free, remote, project-based learning internship in Cyber Security with EdBytes.

Over the next five milestones, I plan to practise phishing indicator triage, salted password hashing in Python, HTTP/DNS packet inspection using Wireshark, and loopback port-scan interpretation using synthetic lab data.

I will document my findings and defensive takeaways as I work through each guided task.

#EdBytes #CyberSecurity #LearningInPublic
```

## Pre-Publish Checklist
- [x] Mentions **EdBytes** and **Cyber Security** domain accurately.
- [x] Describes it strictly as a **remote, project-based learning internship** (no claim of employment, stipend, or guaranteed placement).
- [x] Includes a concrete skill/project goal and `#EdBytes`.
- [x] Contains zero email addresses, phone numbers, or offer-letter contact details.

## How to Submit the Link
1. Open LinkedIn, click **Start a post**, and paste the text above.
2. Make sure the audience selector at the top is set to **Anyone** and click **Post**.
3. Click the three dots (`...`) on your published post and select **Copy link to post** (looks like `https://www.linkedin.com/posts/...` or `https://www.linkedin.com/feed/update/urn:li:activity:...`).
4. Open an **Incognito / Private browser window** and verify the post loads publicly without logging in.
5. Paste that single individual post URL into the EdBytes Milestone 1 submission box (do not paste your profile URL or company page URL).
